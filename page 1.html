Why Chilled?

Chilled is a cozy, cafe that can fit into and adjust to any event you can ask for. We aim to bring comfort and hydration to everyone, whether it be in a special event, or one of our shops.

What we offer

  • Hot or cold drinks every day of the year for any event
  • Options for only hot or cold drinks at your event
  • Services to any county in Florida.
