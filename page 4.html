About Us

What we do:

Welcome to Chilled, where we bring a sense of calm and a perfect cup of coffee directly to your most memorable days. We specialize in bringing our cozy, mobile cafe experience to weddings, corporate events, and birthday celebrations. We believe that the best moments happen when everyone can slow down, connect, and relax. That is why we craft each specialty espresso drink, soothing tea, and signature beverage with care—turning any venue into a warm, welcoming space. Whether you are celebrating a milestone or taking a well-deserved workday break, Chilled is there to help you pause, sip, and savor the moment.
